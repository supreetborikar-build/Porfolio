Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This page explains what information may be processed when you visit this portfolio website, how it is used, and the choices available to you. This site is a personal portfolio and does not currently provide user accounts, a newsletter, or an on-site contact form.

1. Information processed when you visit

When a website is hosted and delivered, the hosting provider may automatically process technical request data such as your IP address, date and time of access, requested page, browser and device information, and error/security logs. These records are generally used to deliver the site, maintain security, and diagnose technical issues. The hosting provider and its retention practices should be identified in the hosting account’s current privacy documentation.

2. Browser storage

This website uses browser local storage to remember interface preferences:

  • portfolio-locale stores the language preference (English or German).
  • portfolio-soundsEnabled stores whether optional website sounds are enabled.

These values are stored on your device and are used to keep your preferences between visits. They are not used by this site to identify you. You can clear them through your browser’s site-data settings; doing so may reset your preferences.

3. External services and links

The site loads the Poppins typeface from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google may receive technical connection information such as your IP address. This portfolio also links to third-party services and project websites, including social networks and hosted project demos. If you follow those links, the third party’s own privacy policy and terms apply. This site does not control their data practices.

4. Analytics, advertising and contact

No analytics or advertising service, user registration, or on-site contact submission service is intentionally configured in this portfolio’s current source. If these features are added later, this policy should be updated before they are enabled.

You can contact me through my LinkedIn profile.

5. Your choices and rights

You can disable or clear local storage through your browser settings and choose whether to follow external links or load external fonts. Depending on your location, applicable privacy laws may provide rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other handling of personal information. To make a privacy request, contact me using the LinkedIn profile above.

6. Children

This portfolio is not designed to collect personal information from children, and it does not offer child-directed accounts or services.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website’s features, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Supreet Borikar through LinkedIn.
